## Visual direction and asset reuse

Retain Last Light's printed-card identity: warm ink, ivory paper, citron, restrained copper, Fraunces for short headings, and Manrope for readable names, ranks and controls. The [current theme](../../composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/dev/partydeck/app/ui/theme/PartyDeckTheme.kt) supplies the final tokens, including the stronger essential-control outline. Existing [design findings](../../docs/design-review.md) and [gameplay requirements](../../docs/research/ui-game.md) supply the readability baseline; each Godot presentation needs fresh evidence.

The 3D owner's bounded orthographic table, restrained key light and readable controls are a suitable starting point. Card faces, names and actions must retain contrast under the actual lighting and camera. The 2D candidate should keep the same information hierarchy. Compare complete playable states at identical window sizes, rather than choosing from a decorative opening scene.

- Original rank/back/mark sources are in `assets/vectors`; `assets/tools/generate_vectors.py` contains their drawing recipes. The four rank SVGs use a 64 × 64 viewBox; the back uses 160 × 240. Inspected game SVGs contain paths/groups, with no text or external references. Reuse within PartyDeck is supported by that provenance; no public-domain or CC0 claim is made.
- The four font binaries and both OFL files independently match [the source manifest](../../assets/font_sources.json). Preserve their copyright notices and SIL OFL 1.1 in the renderer distribution. Keep rank words and UI copy outside texture artwork so text can enlarge and localize.
- All six original WAVE cues independently match [the audio manifest](../../assets/audio_manifest.json), totaling 285,150 bytes. The checked-in generator synthesizes them without external samples. Imported playback, loudness on devices and lifecycle behavior still need review.
- Godot normally rasterizes SVGs at import; 3D use can change mipmap/compression settings [2]. Inspect the imported card-back hairlines, Wild intersections and Moon detail at actual displayed sizes. Source-vector quality alone is insufficient. Any new models/textures/audio need recorded provenance. The Godot runtime adds its own MIT and third-party notices to the existing font/asset obligations [6].

## Renderer acceptance

| Area | Required behavior and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Cards and public table | At a constrained phone width, required rank, current actor, latest named claim and action remain readable together. Each hand card has a distinct shape and rank word. Selected state uses a check/outline plus a count, with meaningful boundaries retaining 3:1 contrast and ordinary text 4.5:1. Show opponents' counts and public fuse state only. Preserve duplicate-name seat disambiguation and inspectable prior-round proof. |
| Selection and touch | Tap toggles a stable card identity; every selected card remains deselectable. A fourth choice retains the first three and gives a count-only explanation. Five cards must not force shrunken targets or overlapping hit regions: reflow or scroll with the fifth card reachable. Require measured host-space targets of at least 48 × 48 dp on Android and our 44 × 44 pt product floor on iOS [7]. Drag, tilt, long press and precision mesh picking cannot be required to play. Pending/rejected actions retain coherent selection and prevent duplicate submission. |
| Reveal and result | Public claims/piles use backs until an authoritative challenge reveals the permitted cards. Show claimant, challenger, rank/count, truthful/bluff verdict, and the named settled Still in/Out result without waiting for animation. Mark mismatches with text/shape as well as color. The six-light fuse never reveals the secret future burnout position. Forced challenge, empty hand, eliminated spectator and winner each expose only their valid actions. |
| Hide and lifecycle privacy | Entry and return begin concealed. Hide clears selection and private per-card labels/bindings, picking hints and accessibility nodes immediately; opacity/rotation alone is insufficient. Generic cached rank artwork is harmless, but private card assignments must not remain exposed. Inactive/leave events override queued snapshots and cancel effects. A native privacy cover must protect the engine surface and any overlay before platform snapshots, with Recents/App Switcher and interrupted-gesture evidence on each platform. A desktop hidden frame does not close this gate. |
| Large text and accessibility | At 200% text, reflow labels, hand and actions without losing state or clipping essential controls. Whole-viewport scaling alone is not this test [4]. A complete visible native controls mode may provide the enlarged layout. Independently require native TalkBack/VoiceOver traversal, selection, deselection, maximum-selection feedback, result/turn announcements, Hide removal and stable focus. No private hand live-region announcements or duplicate engine/overlay controls. See the verified native-support constraint below. |
| Motion, sound and haptics | Respect the effective shell preferences and foreground state. Reduced motion presents the settled state immediately, with no card lift/flip, camera shake, parallax, looping glow or required particle sequence. Normal effects are short and finite; no command or outcome waits for them. One feedback owner prevents duplicate cues across native/engine layers. Muting and background/leave stop playback; reconnecting to an old result does not replay it. Every audible event has visible meaning. |
| Mobile GPU and lifetime | Record the actual renderer, backend, resolution, device and build for measurements. Mobile and Compatibility are the documented candidates for mobile; Forward+ desktop captures cannot stand in for those results [1]. Start with bounded geometry/textures, restrained lighting and no shadow/postprocessing dependency. Review transparency/overdraw, texture filtering/mips and material cost [2][3]. The product target is smooth 60 Hz presentation (16.7 ms frame interval), to be measured on representative Android/iOS hardware: report frame-time distribution/misses, CPU/GPU cost, memory, cold first effects and a sustained warm run. Verify repeated entry/exit has no growing retained resources, off-game render loop or audio. No device-performance claim follows from desktop/software rendering. |

Godot **4.7.2-stable** compiles its AccessKit driver only for `macos`, `windows` and `linuxbsd`; the adapter header contains those three adapters, and the base accessibility server reports unsupported [5]. CanvasLayer controls therefore do not establish mobile screen-reader support. The native integration needs an accessible controls/overlay path consuming the same recipient-safe state and submitting the same intents, or independently verified native engine support. The mobile gate remains open while either prototype is evaluated visually.
